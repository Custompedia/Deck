03 · Target Audience

Who We're
Talking To

Naraya operates in B2B. Every piece of content is designed to answer the unspoken questions of three key decision-maker segments — who use Instagram to shortlist and verify vendors.

🎪
Segment 01
Event Organizer
  • Age 19–45 — students & professionals managing events
  • Need: reliable vendor with good track record & fair price
  • Pain point: hard to find vendors who've handled events at scale
  • Convinced by: portfolio, social media presence, price transparency
🏛️
Segment 02
Government & Ministry
  • Age 30–50 — decision makers for formal government events
  • Need: vendor experienced in government event environments
  • Pain point: hard to find trusted vendors with government track record
  • Convinced by: proven credentials, referrals, past government projects
🏢
Segment 03
Corporate Operations
  • Age 28–45 — managers sourcing vendors for internal events
  • Need: reliable vendor for corporate gathering & internal events
  • Pain point: hard to find a trusted vendor with corporate experience
  • Convinced by: portfolio, track record, pricing fit
Key insight: B2B audiences don't buy from Instagram — they use it to verify. Every post needs to answer one question: "Is Naraya credible enough to trust with our event?"

04 · Strategic Approach

3 Months.
3 Phases. One Goal.

The strategy is built progressively — establishing identity first, deepening trust, then converting attention into real inquiries.

01
JUNE 2026
Brand Positioning & Digital Foundation
Build a strong, consistent first impression. Introduce Naraya as a professional production partner to the right audience — and make that positioning unmistakable from day one.
02
JULY 2026
Engagement & Trust
Deepen audience trust through storytelling, behind-the-setup content, and real proof of Naraya's technical capability and work ethic.
03
AUGUST 2026
Drive Conversion
Convert the trust built over two months into real inquiries. Social proof from prestige clients and project-specific content give the audience everything they need to reach out.
The audience built in month one is the same audience that will DM Naraya in month three. Consistency is what makes it work.

05 · Content Pillars

Content Pillars

Four pillars, each with a specific role in the audience's journey — from discovering Naraya to deciding to reach out.

01
Project Showcase
The story behind each event — scale, setup, execution. Real documentation of Naraya's best work presented with context and pride.
Builds Credibility
02
Behind the Setup
POV of the team working — installation, coordination, technical execution. The content B2B audiences watch to assess how a vendor actually operates.
Builds Connection
03
Technical Education
Content that answers real questions — what to look for in a videotron vendor, how to choose sound systems for different event scales. Saved and shared by B2B audiences.
Builds Authority
04
Social Proof
Testimonials and recognition from past clients — government, corporate, EO. Real words from real clients carry more weight than any caption Naraya can write.
Builds Trust
Pillar composition shifts each month in line with the strategic phase. Breakdown per month is in the following slides.
