04 · Strategic Approach

3 Months.
3 Phases. One Goal.

The strategy is built progressively — establishing awareness first, deepening trust, then driving conversion organically.

01
JUNE 2026
Awareness & Identity
Build a strong, consistent first impression. Introduce Ecolux as a Luxury Bespoke Wood Doors Specialist to the right audience — and make that positioning unmistakable from day one.
02
JULY 2026
Engagement & Trust
Deepen audience trust through storytelling and transparency. Leverage the Indo Build Tech 8–12 July momentum as an organic reach booster and lead attractor.
03
AUGUST 2026
Drive Conversion
Convert the trust built over two months into real inquiries. Product-specific content and social proof from prestigious clients give the audience everything they need to reach out.
All three phases are interconnected — the audience built in month one is the same audience that will send an inquiry in month three. Consistency is what makes it work.
